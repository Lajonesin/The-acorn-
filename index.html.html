Executive Session

Full Board Summary — July 20, 2026

Your Board is committed to keeping owners informed. While our meetings are held in Executive Session, we want residents to know what's being discussed and decided on behalf of the community. Here's a summary of our most recent session, including the extended Owner Q&A on security and break-ins.

🚔 Break-Ins & Vehicle Theft
Increased Overnight Security Presence In Progress

Multiple owners reported recent vehicle break-ins and tire thefts, and the Board acknowledged that similar incidents have been reported at nearby properties. The current guard schedule includes two guards overnight (11 p.m.–7 a.m., one patrolling continuously, one rotating/stationary), and the security service has agreed to increase overnight presence further and resume use of traffic cones at night as an added deterrent. This is a temporary schedule pending further changes.

If You See Something Ongoing

Please call the guard house immediately so guards have a real-time description to act on, and also call 911 (or the HPD non-emergency line) so the incident is on record — repeated calls help the area get taken seriously by HPD. The Board reiterated it cannot afford armed security, and residents should never put themselves at risk confronting a suspect.

Were You a Victim of a Crime? Resource

If your vehicle or property was actually broken into or stolen from, HPD's online reporting tool lets you file an official police report yourself, on your own time — this is for after something has happened to you specifically, not for reporting general suspicious activity (use the alert form above for that).

Patrol Strategy Update Approved

The Board approved shifting from scheduled patrol scans to an initial scan followed by random roaming patrols — making patrol timing less predictable is a deliberate security improvement.

Guardhouse Phone Service Checking Options

AT&T's fiber infrastructure installation is complete. Management is checking availability of AT&T service to address the guardhouse phone periodically going out.

Surveillance Signage

The Board discussed surveillance signage and coordinating with neighboring communities to share security information and camera footage when appropriate.

📷 Security Cameras
Property-Wide Camera Coverage — Gathering Cost Estimates In Progress

The Oaks of Woodlake spans approximately 25 acres, and comprehensive camera coverage has long been considered cost-prohibitive due to competing priorities such as roofing, siding, paving, utilities, insurance, and payroll.

To better understand the costs, the Association has requested proposals for property-wide camera coverage. Once the bids are received and evaluated, the Board will determine whether the project is financially feasible.

In the meantime, owners may install exterior security cameras serving their own units without prior Board approval, provided the cameras are not directed into a neighbor's windows or other private areas.

📣 Reporting Suspicious Activity
Report Everything You See

Please report any and all suspicious activity using the appropriate channel — HPD (911 or non-emergency) and the guard house — and always let the management office know as well, so incidents can be tracked. Maintenance concerns should be submitted to the office through the Buildium/Vantaca portal, whether it's something specific to your unit or a general safety or infrastructure issue in the community's common areas.

100% Insurance Coverage Approved

🎉  Community Update
100%
Replacement-Cost Property Insurance Approved

The Board has approved upgrading the Association's master property insurance policy to full 100% replacement-cost coverage. This is a meaningful step forward for the financial strength and long-term stability of The Oaks.

How We Made This Decision

Our insurance premium was decreasing in the coming year. Rather than bank those savings, the Board made the strategic choice to reinvest that amount into upgrading to 100% property coverage — bringing this stronger protection to the community at essentially no added cost.

Why This Matters

Many traditional mortgage lenders will not finance home purchases in communities that lack adequate property insurance. Without full coverage, potential buyers face closed doors when trying to secure conventional financing.

While achieving full coverage is a critical step forward, it is important to note that it does not automatically qualify our community for FHA loans, which require a separate, comprehensive federal approval process. However, resolving our insurance requirements successfully opens the door to standard conventional financing options that were previously unavailable to our residents.

What This Means for You

  • For Sellers: Prospective buyers gain access to more traditional financing options. This expands your potential buyer pool and directly supports property values.
  • For Buyers: Future purchasers can explore a wider variety of standard conventional mortgages, subject to their personal lender qualifications.
  • For All Residents: Comprehensive coverage safeguards our community against catastrophic financial losses from fire, severe weather, liability claims, and other insurable events.

This update represents a vital strategic investment in the future of The Oaks, protecting our structural integrity and restoring financial flexibility for our residents.
