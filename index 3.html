Group-rate security equipment — interest survey

Following recent security incidents in the community, we're gauging interest in a group rate for fiber-backed security equipment before reaching out to vendors. This takes about 3–4 minutes.

Preview only — not yet collecting responses

This is a draft for board review. Submitting the form below will not save or send anything. Once approved, this will be activated and shared with residents in the newsletter.

Before you begin — please read

  1. No cost to the Association. The HOA is only facilitating this process and doing the initial legwork. It is not purchasing equipment or spending community funds to subsidize or arrange a discount.
  2. No HOA liability or warranties. Any equipment purchase, contract, or monitoring service will be handled directly between you and the vendor. The HOA does not offer, manage, or back any equipment warranty or service guarantee.
  3. Completely voluntary. Participation is 100% optional.
